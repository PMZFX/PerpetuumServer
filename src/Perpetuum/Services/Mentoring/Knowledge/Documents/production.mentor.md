Production is a chain rather than a single button: obtain the relevant production data or calibration template, meet its extension requirements, gather the listed materials and components, and use the appropriate terminal facility. Prototyping, reverse engineering, and mass production are different stages. Exact recipes and efficiencies must be queried from the current server before planning a build.
