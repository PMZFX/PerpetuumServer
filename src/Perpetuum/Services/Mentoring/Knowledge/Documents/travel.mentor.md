Terminals provide docking services, while teleporters connect specific destinations. Use landmarks and the world map to identify a destination, then verify every teleport leg instead of assuming two zones connect directly. Mission coordinates describe a point inside a zone; they are not by themselves a complete inter-zone route, and Mentor will say when it lacks verified routing data.
