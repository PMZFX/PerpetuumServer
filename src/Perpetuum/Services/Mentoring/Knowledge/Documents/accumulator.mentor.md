The accumulator is your robot's energy reserve. Active modules consume it, while the robot restores it over time. If modules stop or refuse to cycle, let the accumulator recover, deactivate unnecessary modules, and check each module's energy use; fitting and extension choices can improve capacity or recharge, but exact values should be read from the current item information.
