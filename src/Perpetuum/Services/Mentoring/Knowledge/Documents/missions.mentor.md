A mission can contain several ordered or simultaneous objectives. Use the live active objective, its localized server instruction, target, progress, and coordinates before giving procedural advice. The mission display and surface landmarks remain the player-facing source for the exact destination.

For an Item Supply objective, approach and activate the marked Item Supply object, then remain still while its timed operation completes; the supplied mission item is placed into robot cargo. For a Submit Item objective, approach and open the marked submission object, open robot cargo, and move the required item into the Submit items window. Do not call either interaction complete merely because the robot reached its landmark.

If an objective appears complete but does not advance, verify its exact action, destination, required cargo space, and required item before suggesting that the player abandon the mission.
