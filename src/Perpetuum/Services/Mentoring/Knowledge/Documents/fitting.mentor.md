Fit equipment while docked. A module must match an available slot and the robot's fitting rules, and the completed fit must stay within CPU and powergrid limits. Required extensions can also block fitting or effective use; inspect the module information for its exact slot, resource use, and prerequisites rather than assuming similarly named equipment is interchangeable.
