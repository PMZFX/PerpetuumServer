Mining has two distinct jobs: locate a mineral deposit with suitable scanning equipment, then extract it with a compatible mining module and charge. Lock or designate the valid deposit as required, move into module range, and watch cargo capacity and accumulator energy. Exact mineral locations, module compatibility, and yields must come from current server data rather than a historical guide.
